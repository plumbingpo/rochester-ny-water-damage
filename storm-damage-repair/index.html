Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
