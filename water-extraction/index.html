Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Rochester Restoration Co provides emergency water extraction throughout Rochester using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • A clearly labeled main water valve, in a spot everyone knows, saves time in an emergency
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
