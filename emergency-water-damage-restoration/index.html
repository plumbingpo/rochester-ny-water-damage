Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.

Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
